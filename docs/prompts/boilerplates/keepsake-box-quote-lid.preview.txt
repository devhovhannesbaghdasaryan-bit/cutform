Style: engraved quote lid.
- The MANUFACTURING ARTWORK is a black-on-white typographic composition. On the box every black mark is burnt into the inside face of the lid as dark burnt-brown engraving in the light oak-toned wood; every white area is plain wood. Reproduce it one-to-one: same words, letterforms, line order, sizes and centred placement, nothing added, removed or re-typeset.
- Construction from the PRODUCT TEMPLATE: a rectangular oak-toned wooden box, its lid open and tilted back on two small bronze hinges at the back, a small bronze clasp centred on the front edge, the inside of the box empty. The template's engraved quote is the same design; the names, date and rings emblem on its front face and the props inside it are placeholders and must not appear.
- Personalized text, if given, is laser-engraved once on the front face of the box, centred below the clasp: wide-spaced serif capitals in the same dark burnt-brown engraving, broken into lines only where the customer broke it, the first line largest, and a small interlocking double-ring emblem engraved centred beneath the last line. The text never appears inside the lid. Without text the front face stays plain wood, with no emblem.
