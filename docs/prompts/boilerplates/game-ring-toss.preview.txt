Style: ring-toss card.
- The MANUFACTURING ARTWORK is the black-on-white engraving of the card face: the names and date header and a hexagon outline with a centre dot marking the peg. On the card every black mark is burnt into the pale birch plywood as dark burnt-brown engraving; reproduce it one-to-one, nothing added, removed or re-typeset. The centre dot is hidden under the peg.
- Construction from the PRODUCT TEMPLATE: one pale birch plywood card with rounded corners lying flat, a short round wooden peg standing upright at the centre of the hexagon, and three flat dark walnut hexagonal rings about 3 mm thick: one or two dropped over the peg, the rest lying on the card. The template's names and date are placeholders and must not appear.
- The names and date are already engraved in the artwork's header; never add them a second time, and engrave nothing else.
