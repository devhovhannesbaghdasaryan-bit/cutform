Production file for the engraved face of a laser-engraved wooden "Our Story" timeline plaque. Draw the exact engraving artwork the CO2 laser will raster-engrave into the plywood: the title, the couple's names, a winding timeline path with one milestone per event, a small illustration beside each milestone, botanical sprigs in the corners and a closing phrase. There is no customer photo, and nothing but this artwork is engraved on the plaque.

Output: a flat, square, black-on-white image — solid pure black marks on a pure white background and nothing else. Every black pixel is burnt into the wood; every white pixel stays untouched wood.

Canvas: the square canvas is the plaque at true scale: a landscape rectangle that spans the full canvas width and the middle 70% of the canvas height, centred. The plaque itself is never drawn: no outline, rounded corners, hanging holes, cord or wood grain. The canvas above and below the plaque stays pure white.

Fixed elements, always drawn:
- The title Our Story in an elegant high-contrast serif, upper and lower case, centred near the top of the plaque, about 9% of the plaque height tall, its thinnest hairline at least 0.4% of the image width thick. A strip along the top edge of the plaque, 6% of the plaque height tall, stays empty for the hanging holes.
- A small botanical sprig of line art (one stem, a few leaves, a berry or two) in each of the four corners of the plaque and one on each side of the names, all strokes one uniform weight of about 0.4% of the image width.
- The timeline path: one continuous smooth line of the same weight that starts at the left edge of the plaque just below the names, sweeps across the plaque in three or four wide S-curves and ends at the lower right above the closing phrase. It never crosses itself and never touches any text.
- The closing phrase ...and the best is yet to come! in a flowing calligraphic script in the lower-right corner of the plaque, about 5% of the plaque height tall, letterforms solid filled black with smooth closed edges.
