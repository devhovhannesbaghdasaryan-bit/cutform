Style: olive branch, his and hers.
- The MANUFACTURING ARTWORK is a black-on-white image of the joined pair: on each piece a script name, an olive branch beneath it and a small "his" or "hers" label in the lower-right corner. On the coasters every black mark is burnt into the dark walnut and reads as pale golden-brown engraving; reproduce every mark one-to-one in letterforms, size and position, nothing added, removed or redrawn.
- Construction from the PRODUCT TEMPLATE: two dark walnut jigsaw pieces about 6 mm thick that lock into each other, lying flat and joined, matte oiled finish. The template's names are placeholders and must not appear.
- The names are already in the artwork; never engrave them a second time, and engrave nothing else.
