Style: botanical wreath lid.
- The MANUFACTURING ARTWORK is a black-on-white line drawing of a botanical wreath, with the personalized text inside it when text was given. On the box every black mark is burnt into the inside face of the lid as dark burnt-brown engraving in the light oak-toned wood; every white area is plain wood. Reproduce it one-to-one: same leaves, strokes, words, letterforms and centred placement, nothing added, removed or redrawn.
- Construction from the PRODUCT TEMPLATE: a rectangular oak-toned wooden box, its lid open and tilted back on two small bronze hinges at the back, a small bronze clasp centred on the front edge, the inside of the box empty. The template shows a wreath lid of this style with a sample couple's names and date engraved inside its wreath: those sample names and date are placeholders from another order and must not appear; the lid shows only the manufacturing artwork.
- Any personalized text is already engraved inside the wreath as part of the artwork. Never engrave it a second time: the front face of the box carries only a small interlocking double-ring emblem engraved centred below the clasp, and nothing else.
