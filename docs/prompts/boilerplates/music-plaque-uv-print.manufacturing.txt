Production file for a UV-printed clear acrylic night light shaped as a music-player plaque: a portrait panel that shows the customer's photo like album art, with the song title, the artist, a player bar with controls and a scan-code strip beneath it. Turn the attached customer photo and the text below into the exact print file for the panel.

Output: a flat, square, print-ready image. The square canvas holds the portrait panel at true proportion: a centred rectangle spanning the full canvas height and the middle 72% of the canvas width. The canvas outside the panel stays solid pure black and is not printed.

Print convention (the printer reads it exactly this way):
- Solid pure black (#000000) means clear, unprinted acrylic. The whole panel background is pure black, with no gradient, vignette, texture, glow or noise anywhere.
- The photo is printed in full colour. Every other element is pure white (#FFFFFF) with crisp edges and is printed in white ink; no grey, no anti-aliasing haze, no shadows, no second colour.

Layout inside the panel, all measurements relative to the panel height, everything centred on the panel's vertical axis:
- Photo: a square with rounded corners (corner radius 7% of its width), 72% of the panel width wide, its top edge 7% down from the top of the panel. It holds exactly the people (or pet or object) in the customer photo: same number, pose, identity, facial features, expression, hairstyle and clothing. Never add, remove, duplicate or mirror anyone, and never change faces or body shapes. Keep the photo's real scene, recomposed into a square crop centred on the subjects; if the photo is too narrow, extend only its existing background naturally and never invent new objects or people. Gentle photo finishing only: balanced exposure, natural colour, clean skin tones, crisp focus and slightly richer contrast for printing on clear acrylic; no painterly, cartoon, filter or vintage effects. Outside the rounded square the background stays pure black.
- Title and artist space: the band from 4% to 12% below the photo (see Text below); when no text is described it stays pure black.
- Progress bar: a horizontal white line 0.4% of the panel height thick spanning 72% of the panel width, 15% below the photo, with a solid white round knob 2% of the panel height across sitting on the line at 60% of its length; the part of the line left of the knob is 0.8% thick.
- Controls row: 4% below the progress bar, five white glyphs evenly spread across the bar's width, each about 4% of the panel height tall: a heart outline, a previous-track symbol (two solid left-pointing triangles), a pause symbol (two solid vertical bars) in the centre, a next-track symbol (two solid right-pointing triangles) and a repeat symbol (two arrows forming a loop).
- Code strip: 5% below the controls, 72% of the panel width wide and 9% of the panel height tall: at its left end a solid white circle 9% of the panel height across with a black headphones glyph cut out of it, and to its right, filling the rest of the strip, 23 solid white vertical bars of one even width with equal gaps, their heights varying irregularly between 20% and 100% of the strip height and vertically centred on the strip's middle line. The bars are a decorative scan-code pattern and must not form letters or a barcode of a real product.
- Below the code strip the panel stays pure black down to the bottom edge; the bottom 8% of the panel sits in the base and carries no print.
