Engraving treatment — LINE MODE. This directive is mandatory and overrides the default artwork treatment; everything else follows the product prompt without deviation.

Render the subject of the customer photo on the acrylic as engraved contour line art, exactly as if the laser file were an SVG whose <path> elements use a single stroke and no fill:
- Every mark is a clean continuous line of one uniform weight, about 1% of the artwork width. No thick strokes, no variable-weight brush lines.
- Forbidden inside the artwork: solid filled regions, photographic tones, sepia or grayscale shading, gradients, halftone dots, stippling, hatching or cross-hatching.
- Lines follow only the real edges and features of the customer photo: outer silhouette, hair mass, facial features (eyes, brows, nose, mouth, beard), hands, and the main clothing folds, seams and pockets.
- One uniform engraving tone: every line glows the same warm amber; untouched acrylic stays clear.
- The drawing is a tracing of the customer photo, not of the product template: exactly the same number of people as the customer photo (one person stays one person), same pose, same proportions. The template's sample artwork only illustrates line quality and must not be reproduced.
- Do not add, beautify or reinterpret anything; do not introduce any person or element that is not visible in the customer photo.
