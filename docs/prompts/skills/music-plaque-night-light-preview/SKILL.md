# Music-plaque night-light preview: rendering rules

## What the attached images are
- MANUFACTURING ARTWORK: the exact print file of this order for the portrait panel, already generated from the customer's photo and text. It is the only source of what is on the panel: the photo, the title, the artist, the player bar, the control glyphs and the code strip.
- PRODUCT TEMPLATE: an example lamp of the selected style. It fixes construction only: portrait panel, base, cable and lighting. Its dashed photo frame, placeholder photo icon and the words "Your Photo Here", "Song Title" and "Artist Name" are placeholders and never appear in the result.

## How to read the print file (highest priority)
- Solid black in the artwork is clear, unprinted acrylic: on the lamp those areas are transparent, with the dim room faintly visible through them.
- The photo inside the rounded square is a full-colour print: same subjects and the same number of them, same faces, poses, crop and proportions, in the same place on the panel.
- Every white element is white ink that glows softly in the LED colour: same words, letterforms, glyphs, bar pattern and positions.
- No redrawing, restyling, beautifying, simplifying, recolouring or extending of the artwork; no added decorations, dates, names, lyrics, timestamps, logos or brand marks; no second copy of any text.

## Construction (from the product template)
- A portrait clear acrylic panel, about 3 mm thick, with slightly rounded corners, standing upright in a rectangular light-wood (beech) bar base; a thin USB cable leaves the base on the right.
- The panel is seen from the front, square to the camera, so the whole print is readable; the lower edge of the panel sits in the base.
- Only this construction: never a square or round panel, never a contour-cut silhouette, never a different base.

## Light
- Supported LED colours: warm white #f7d7a1 (default), soft amber #f4bf73, soft peach #f5b49f, mint #a8dbc2, sky blue #9fcaea.
- The LED in the base lights the panel from below: its edges glow in the requested colour and the white print glows evenly in the same tint; the photo keeps its natural colours. No harsh glare, lens flares or other colours.

## Personalized text
- The song title and artist are already printed in the artwork beneath the photo. Reproduce them character-for-character: never translated, corrected, reworded, abbreviated or hyphenated, never moved, never repeated anywhere else.
- The wooden base stays plain: nothing is engraved or printed on it.

## Scene
- Photorealistic photo of the lit lamp on a nightstand, desk or shelf in a cozy, dim room with a softly blurred background; the lamp centred and fully in frame; no hands, no people.
- Never show the customer photo or the artwork file as an inset, collage or second image. No logos, labels or watermarks.

## Self-check before finishing
1. The panel shows exactly the manufacturing artwork: the same photo in the rounded square, the same title and artist, the same glyphs and code strip, nothing from the template.
2. Black areas of the artwork are clear acrylic; white elements glow in the LED colour; the photo is in full colour.
3. Construction matches the template: portrait panel, rounded corners, light-wood base, cable on the right.
4. No inset, no extra text, no added decoration, and the base is plain.
