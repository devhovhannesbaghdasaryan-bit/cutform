# Contour night-light preview: rendering rules

## What the attached images are
- MANUFACTURING ARTWORK: the black-on-white line drawing the laser will engrave, already generated from the customer's photo. It is the only source of the artwork: who is shown, how many, their poses, faces, hair and clothing all come from it.
- PRODUCT TEMPLATE: fixes the construction of the lamp only. The couple engraved on its panel is a placeholder sample from another order: its people, their number, pose, composition and panel outline never appear in the result.

## Artwork fidelity (highest priority)
- Every line of the manufacturing artwork appears on the panel one-to-one: same position, proportion and stroke weight. Nothing is redrawn, smoothed, thickened, simplified or reinterpreted.
- Nothing is added: no extra lines, shading, fills, people, accessories, hairstyles or scenery. Nothing is removed.
- The number of people on the lamp equals the number in the manufacturing artwork.

## Product construction (from the product template)
- A clear acrylic panel cut along the outer silhouette of the manufacturing artwork with a small even margin, standing upright; its straight bottom edge sits in the base slot.
- An oval light-wood (beech) base with a slot the panel sits in, and a thin white USB cable leaving the base on the right.
- The base's LED edge-lights the panel: the engraved lines glow in the LED colour (warm amber when no colour is given); clear, unengraved acrylic stays transparent with only faint edge highlights. White areas of the artwork are never rendered as a white or frosted surface.

## Scene and framing
- Photorealistic product photo of the lit lamp on a wooden desk, warm ambient light, softly blurred interior behind it, lamp centred and fully in frame.
- Do not show the customer's photo or the artwork file anywhere in the image (no inset, no collage). Only the lamp is shown.

## Text
- If personalized text is provided, laser-engrave it once on the front face of the wooden base: centred horizontally, a single line, dark burnt-brown lettering cut into the wood grain, sized to fit the base with even margins, reproduced character-for-character.
- The text is engraved wood, so it does not glow and is never placed on the acrylic panel.
- Without personalized text the base stays plain wood.
- No other text, logos, labels or watermarks anywhere in the image.

## Self-check before finishing
1. The panel shows exactly the manufacturing artwork's lines, nothing added or removed, and nobody from the product template.
2. The panel outline follows the artwork's silhouette with a small even margin.
3. Construction matches the product template: oval wooden base, white cable on the right.
4. Only glowing lines on clear acrylic; no photographic, shaded or filled rendering.
5. Only the requested text, engraved once on the wooden base; nothing else written.
