# Keepsake-box preview: rendering rules

## What the attached images are
- MANUFACTURING ARTWORK: the exact black-on-white engraving file for the inside of the lid, already generated from the customer's order. It is the only source of what is engraved inside the lid: every word, letterform, leaf and line comes from it.
- PRODUCT TEMPLATE: an example box of the selected style. It fixes construction only: wood, finish, proportions, hinges, clasp and how the lid opens. Its engraving (quote, names, date, emblem) and any props inside it are placeholders from another order and never appear in the result.

## Fidelity to the manufacturing artwork (highest priority)
- The inside of the lid shows the artwork one-to-one: same words in the same order, same letterforms and line breaks, same proportions and centred placement.
- No redrawing, restyling, re-typesetting, correcting, translating, simplifying or extending of the artwork; no added ornaments, hearts, rings, flourishes, borders or extra words.
- The artwork keeps its own margins to the lid edges; nothing runs under the hinges or off the lid.

## Engraving look
- Every black mark of the artwork is burnt into the wood: matte, dark burnt-brown, slightly darker at its edges, sunk very slightly below the surface, with the wood grain faintly visible through it.
- Every white area is plain, untouched wood. Engraving never glows, is never printed in colour, and is never raised, painted, gilded or inlaid.

## Construction (from the product template)
- A rectangular wooden box with an oak-toned satin finish and visible straight grain; a lid of the same wood on two small bronze butt hinges at the back; a small bronze clasp centred on the front edge.
- The box stands with its front towards the camera and the lid open and tilted back, so the whole inside of the lid faces the camera and the engraving is fully readable.
- The inside of the box is empty plain wood: no envelope, feather, rings, flowers or other props.

## Personalized text
- Reproduced character-for-character: never translated, corrected, reworded, abbreviated or hyphenated; line breaks only where the customer broke the text; shrink to fit, never truncate.
- Placed only where the selected style says: inside the lid as part of the artwork, or engraved on the front face. Never twice, and never on both surfaces.
- Front-face text, when the style puts it there: centred below the clasp, wide-spaced serif capitals in the same dark burnt-brown engraving, the first line largest, and the small interlocking double-ring emblem engraved centred beneath the last line.
- When the order has no text, the front face stays plain wood unless the style instruction gives it a fixed emblem.

## Scene
- Photorealistic product photo in soft natural daylight on a light lace tablecloth or a pale wooden table, with a gently blurred neutral background; the box centred and fully in frame, no hands, no people.
- Never show the artwork file as an inset, collage or second image. No logos, labels or watermarks.

## Self-check before finishing
1. The inside of the lid shows exactly the manufacturing artwork: every word and line present, nothing added, nothing from the template.
2. Construction matches the template: oak-toned box, two bronze hinges at the back, bronze clasp at the front, lid open and readable, inside empty.
3. Personalized text appears once, verbatim, only where the style defines; the front face is plain when neither the style nor the order gives it anything.
4. All engraving is dark burnt wood; no glow, colour, inset or extra text anywhere.
