# Table-game-card preview: rendering rules

## What the attached images are
- MANUFACTURING ARTWORK: the exact black-on-white engraving file for the card face, already generated from the customer's order. It is the only source of what is engraved: the names and date header, the grid, the rules, the icons, every letter and line.
- PRODUCT TEMPLATE: an example card of the selected game. It fixes construction only: pale birch plywood, rounded corners, the loose wooden pieces that belong to this game, and how the card lies. Its names, date and engraving are placeholders from another order and never appear in the result.

## Fidelity to the manufacturing artwork (highest priority)
- The card face shows the artwork one-to-one: same header, same grid, same words, numbers, icons and line breaks, same proportions and placement.
- No redrawing, restyling, re-typesetting, correcting, translating, simplifying or extending of the artwork; no added ornaments, extra words, extra cells or extra rules.
- The artwork keeps its own margins to the card edges; nothing runs off the card.

## Engraving look
- Every black mark of the artwork is burnt into the plywood: matte, dark burnt-brown, slightly darker at its edges, sunk very slightly below the surface, with the pale grain visible through it.
- Every white area is plain, untouched pale wood. Engraving never glows, is never printed in colour, and is never raised, painted or inlaid.

## Construction (from the product template)
- One rectangular card of pale birch plywood, about 3 mm thick, with rounded corners and clean laser-cut edges that are slightly darker than the face.
- Only the loose pieces the style instruction names are shown, placed on the card as it says; no pieces from other games, no second card.
- The card lies flat on the table, centred, seen from slightly above with the whole face readable.

## Personalized text
- The names and date are already engraved in the artwork's header: reproduce them character-for-character from the artwork and never add them a second time anywhere.
- Never translate, correct, reword, abbreviate or hyphenate; break lines only where the artwork breaks them.

## Scene
- Photorealistic product photo on an elegantly set table in warm soft light: light linen, a candle or glass softly blurred in the background, nothing that hides the card.
- Never show the artwork file as an inset, collage or second image. No logos, labels or watermarks, no hands, no people.

## Self-check before finishing
1. The card face shows exactly the manufacturing artwork: every letter, cell and icon present, nothing added, nothing from the template.
2. Only this game's pieces are on the card, as the style instruction says.
3. The names and date appear once, in the artwork's header, verbatim.
4. All engraving is dark burnt wood on pale plywood; no glow, colour, inset or extra text anywhere.
