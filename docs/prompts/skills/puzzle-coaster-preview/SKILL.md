# Puzzle-coaster preview: rendering rules

## What the attached images are
- MANUFACTURING ARTWORK: the exact black-on-white engraving file for the two coasters lying joined side by side, already generated from the customer's order. It is the only source of what is engraved: each name, each branch, each small label, every letterform.
- PRODUCT TEMPLATE: an example pair of the selected style. It fixes construction only: dark walnut, jigsaw shapes that lock together, thickness and finish. Its names and engraving are placeholders from another order and never appear in the result.

## Fidelity to the manufacturing artwork (highest priority)
- Each coaster shows its part of the artwork one-to-one: same name, same branch, same small label, same positions and proportions, nothing added, removed or re-typeset.
- No ornaments, dates, hearts or extra words beyond what the artwork contains; no second copy of a name anywhere.

## Engraving look
- Every black mark of the artwork is burnt into the dark walnut and reads as pale golden-brown, lighter than the surrounding wood, matte and very slightly sunk, with the grain faintly visible through it.
- Everything else is plain dark walnut with a matte oiled finish. Engraving never glows, is never printed in colour, and is never raised, painted or inlaid.

## Construction (from the product template)
- Two square-ish jigsaw pieces of solid dark walnut about 6 mm thick and about 100 mm across, each with the tabs and notches that make them lock into each other: the left piece's tab fits the right piece's notch.
- The pair lies flat, joined together, centred, seen from slightly above with both faces readable.

## Personalized text
- The two names are already in the artwork: the first name on the left piece, the second on the right piece. Reproduce them character-for-character and never add them a second time.
- Never translate, correct, reword, abbreviate or hyphenate.

## Scene
- Photorealistic product photo on a light linen tablecloth in soft daylight, a glass softly blurred nearby, nothing covering the coasters.
- Never show the artwork file as an inset, collage or second image. No logos, labels or watermarks, no hands, no people.

## Self-check before finishing
1. Each coaster carries exactly its part of the manufacturing artwork, nothing else, nothing from the template.
2. Construction matches the template: two dark walnut jigsaw pieces locked together.
3. Names appear once each, verbatim, on their own piece.
4. All engraving is pale burnt marks on dark walnut; no glow, colour, inset or extra text anywhere.
