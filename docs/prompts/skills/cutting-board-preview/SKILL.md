# Cutting-board preview: rendering rules

## What the attached images are
- MANUFACTURING ARTWORK: the exact black-on-white engraving file for the nested pair seen from the front, already generated from the customer's order. It is the only source of what is engraved: the name on the left board and the name on the right board, every letterform.
- PRODUCT TEMPLATE: an example pair of the selected style. It fixes construction only: wood, shape, handle hole, wavy joint, heart cutout and finish. Its names are placeholders from another order and never appear in the result.

## Fidelity to the manufacturing artwork (highest priority)
- Each board shows its name one-to-one: same letterforms, same size, same position near the bottom of the board, nothing added, removed or re-typeset.
- No ornaments, dates, hearts or extra words beyond what the artwork contains; no second copy of a name anywhere.

## Engraving look
- Every black mark of the artwork is burnt into the hardwood: matte, dark burnt-brown, slightly darker at its edges, sunk very slightly below the surface, grain visible through it.
- Everything else is plain oiled wood. Engraving never glows, is never printed in colour, and is never raised, painted or inlaid.

## Construction (from the product template)
- Two solid hardwood boards about 20 mm thick: a taller board with a round handle hole at the top, a shorter board beside it with a smaller round handle hole, both with gently flared sides. Their shared edge is a wavy joint, and where it meets the boards a heart is cut through, formed by both boards together.
- The two boards differ slightly in tone (one paler, one more honey-coloured), with visible straight grain and an oiled satin finish.
- The pair stands upright, nested together, leaning against a plain light wall on a dark wooden counter, seen straight on.

## Personalized text
- The two names are already in the artwork: the first name on the left (taller) board, the second on the right board. Reproduce them character-for-character and never add them a second time.
- Never translate, correct, reword, abbreviate or hyphenate.

## Scene
- Photorealistic product photo in soft daylight; a few fresh vegetables or a potted herb may sit nearby, softly blurred and never covering a name.
- Never show the artwork file as an inset, collage or second image. No logos, labels or watermarks, no hands, no people.

## Self-check before finishing
1. Each board carries exactly its name from the manufacturing artwork, nothing else, nothing from the template.
2. Construction matches the template: tall and short board, handle holes, wavy joint, heart cutout, oiled hardwood.
3. Names appear once each, verbatim, near the bottom of their boards.
4. All engraving is dark burnt wood; no glow, colour, inset or extra text anywhere.
