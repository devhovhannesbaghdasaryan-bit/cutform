# Our-story plaque preview: rendering rules

## What the attached images are
- MANUFACTURING ARTWORK: the exact black-on-white engraving file for the plaque's whole face, already generated from the customer's order. It is the only source of what is engraved: the title, the names, the path, every dot, date, label, illustration, sprig and the closing phrase.
- PRODUCT TEMPLATE: an example plaque of the selected style. It fixes construction only: pale birch plywood, rounded corners, two hanging holes with a cord, how it hangs on the wall. Its title, names, milestones and illustrations are placeholders from another order in another language and never appear in the result.

## Fidelity to the manufacturing artwork (highest priority)
- The plaque face shows the artwork one-to-one: same words in the same order and the same places, same letterforms and line breaks, same path shape, same number of dots, same illustrations, same proportions.
- No redrawing, restyling, re-typesetting, correcting, translating, simplifying or extending of the artwork; no added ornaments, hearts, dates, labels or extra words; no milestone dropped or moved.
- The artwork keeps its own margins to the plaque edges; nothing runs into the hanging holes or off the plaque.

## Engraving look
- Every black mark of the artwork is burnt into the plywood: matte, dark burnt-brown, slightly darker at its edges, sunk very slightly below the surface, with the pale grain faintly visible through it.
- Every white area is plain, untouched pale plywood. Engraving never glows, is never printed in colour, and is never raised, painted, gilded or inlaid.

## Construction (from the product template)
- A landscape rectangular plaque of pale birch plywood about 4 mm thick, about 400 mm wide and 280 mm tall, with rounded corners and a smooth sanded face.
- Two small round holes near the top edge, about a third of the width in from each side, with a thin natural cord threaded through them and knotted, by which the plaque hangs.
- The plaque hangs flat against the wall, seen straight on, so the whole face is readable with no perspective distortion.

## Personalized text
- The names and the milestones are already in the artwork: the names under the title, each milestone's date and label beside its dot. Reproduce them character-for-character and never add them a second time.
- Never translate, correct, reword, abbreviate or hyphenate; never reorder milestones.
- When the artwork has no names or milestones, the plaque shows only the fixed title, sprigs, path and closing phrase.

## Scene
- Photorealistic product photo of the plaque hanging on a pale, softly lit wall in natural daylight, dried pampas grass or a similar dried-flower arrangement softly blurred at one side, nothing covering the plaque.
- Never show the artwork file as an inset, collage or second image. No logos, labels or watermarks, no hands, no people.

## Self-check before finishing
1. The plaque face shows exactly the manufacturing artwork: every word, dot and illustration present, nothing added, nothing from the template.
2. Construction matches the template: pale plywood, rounded corners, two holes with a cord, hanging flat on the wall.
3. Names and milestones appear once each, verbatim, in the artwork's places.
4. All engraving is dark burnt marks on pale plywood; no glow, colour, inset or extra text anywhere.
